 Future Demand — Kya Ho Sakta Hai? 
 

 May 2026 ke real data se — agle 3-5 saalon mein Teeli ke liye kya forces kaam karenge: 

 
 
 
 📈 Tailwind #1 — AI-Generated 3D Models Explosion 
 NVIDIA GET3D, Tripo AI, Luma AI — ye tools millions of 3D models generate kar rahe hain. Problem: AI-generated meshes often non-manifold hote hain, broken topology hoti hai. Jitna zyada AI 3D models generate karega, utna zyada repair demand badhegi. Teeli ke liye yeh ek massive hidden tailwind hai — unka core repair pipeline. 
 
 
 📈 Tailwind #2 — E-commerce 3D Mandate 
 Amazon, IKEA, Wayfair — sab 3D product visualization move kar rahe hain. Photorealistic product renders in e-commerce: 94% more customer engagement, 40% fewer returns (industry data 2025). India mein D2C brands bhi is trend mein aa rahe hain. Teeli = perfect tool for product renders without hiring a 3D artist. 
 
 
 📈 Tailwind #3 — Architecture Boom (India + MEA) 
 India smart city projects, UAE mega projects, Saudi Vision 2030 — government-led infrastructure spending driving 3D visualization demand . Architects need renders for tenders, approvals. Teeli ka fast turnaround (upload → render in minutes) is exactly what deadline-driven architects need. 
 
 
 
 
 📈 Tailwind #4 — Real-time Rendering Goes Mainstream 
 NVIDIA Blackwell platform: real-time AI rendering at 25x less cost than previous gen. GPU costs dropping = cloud render farms becoming cheaper = more users can afford Teeli's service. Market expanding downmarket to more users. 
 
 
 📈 Tailwind #5 — Blender Community Explosion 
 3+ million Blender downloads per month (2026). Blender 4.x ke saath zyada professional features aa gaye. Blender users = Teeli's natural market. Sab Blender mein kaam karte hain, rendering ke liye GPU chahiye — Teeli bridges that gap. 
 
 
 📈 Tailwind #6 — Metaverse / AR / VR Assets 
 72% immersive tech developers incorporate 3D assets. 750,000+ custom avatars/environments in virtual worlds in Q4 2023 alone. GLB format = web standard. Teeli ka gltf-transform + Draco pipeline for web-optimized GLB = perfect for metaverse/AR use case.

 07 
 Risks &amp; Challenges — Honest Assessment 
 

 Ek product architect ki tarah — jo risks ignore karta hai wo sirf hype deta hai. Ye hain real risks: 

 
 
 Medium 
 
 Render Farms Add Mesh Repair 
 iRender, GarageFarm, RebusFarm could add a basic mesh repair layer. But: they are infrastructure companies, not software companies. Mesh repair requires ML/geometry expertise they don't have. Timeline: 12-18 months minimum, and they'll do it poorly. Teeli's defense: Speed to market + quality of repair pipeline. 
 
 
 
 Medium 
 
 Tripo AI / AI Companies Expand to Rendering 
 Tripo AI currently does repair but no rendering. If they add GPU rendering, they become a closer competitor. Mitigation: Teeli's architecture-first focus, Blender photorealistic quality vs AI shortcuts. Teeli's renders = production-grade, Tripo's would be "good enough." 
 
 
 
 Lower 
 
 AI Generates Perfect Meshes (Repair Demand Drops) 
 Long-term risk: if AI 3D generators consistently produce clean meshes, repair demand drops. Reality check: Even today's best AI models need cleanup. This risk is 5+ years away. Short-term: AI models actually increasing repair demand. 
 
 
 
 High 
 
 Distribution / User Acquisition Cost 
 Render farms have years of SEO, community presence, plugin ecosystems. Getting Blender artists to switch to a new tool requires trust. Biggest challenge: Distribution, not product. Solution: Blender plugin, community presence on r/blender, ArtStation, CG Cookie, free tier. 
 
 
 
 Medium 
 
 GPU Cost Spike / Modal Pricing Changes 
 If Modal's pricing changes or GPU costs spike, Teeli's unit economics break. Mitigation: Teeli's tech stack (Blender EEVEE for quick renders = very cheap GPU time) + smart caching. EEVEE renders cost pennies on A10G. 
 
 
 
 Lower 
 
 Pricing Race to Bottom 
 Cloud rendering is becoming commoditized. But Teeli isn't selling rendering — selling repair+render pipeline. The repair part has defensible moat. As long as mesh repair stays in the value prop, Teeli can maintain pricing.

 Ek Product Architect Ki Specific Recommendations 

 
 01 Distribution ko underestimate mat karo — Community First 
 Teeli ka sabse bada risk product nahi, distribution hai. Blender plugin banana zaroori hai — renderer forums, r/blender, CGSociety, ArtStation. Free tier critical hai: 2-3 renders/month free rakho. Ek satisfied Blender artist = 10 more through word-of-mouth. Community trust build karna product build karne se zyada important hai is stage par. 
 

 
 02 Focus karo Freelance Architects par — Highest LTV Segment 
 India + Middle East ke freelance architects are highest LTV users : $50-150/month, consistent volume, deadline-driven. Render fail = money lost, client unhappy. Teeli ka "98% success rate" directly solves their biggest pain. Case studies banao: "Architect saves 4 hours per project." Pricing page mein Architecture use-case pehle raho. 
 

 
 03 E-commerce Vertical mein Move karo — Phase 2 
 India mein D2C brands, Meesho sellers, Amazon FBA sellers — inko 3D product renders chahiye but koi affordable, easy tool nahi hai. "Upload your product file, get 360° renders in minutes" — yeh ek standalone product ban sakta hai. SKU-level batch rendering (50 products = 50 renders) is a high-value B2B pitch. 
 

 
 04 API Strategy — Long-term Defensibility 
 API expose karo. 3D printing shops, architecture firms, game asset studios — sab ko batch processing chahiye. API = B2B pipeline integration = higher ACVs + stickier customers. Render farm companies do NOT have good APIs for mesh repair. First-mover advantage here is real.

 ❌ Enterprise mein mat jaao abhi — sales cycle too long, product not proven at scale yet.

 ❌ Animation/VFX mein abhi mat jaao — too expensive, already crowded (GridMarkets, RebusFarm).
